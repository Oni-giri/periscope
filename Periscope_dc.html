Story 01 of 06 · Tuesday, 15 July 2026

Anthropic and DeepMind publish parallel interpretability results on feature circuits

Two labs independently reported that mid-layer feature circuits generalise across model scales. Researchers on your list are split on whether this validates the linear-representation hypothesis or merely reflects shared training data. Three long threads walk through the methodology; the strongest disagreement is over whether the circuits are causal or correlational.

7 tweets · 3 threads · 7 accounts · 06:10–09:55 UTC
CO Chris Olah @ch402 · 06:10 · thread, 9 posts

New paper day. We looked at whether the feature circuits we found in small models persist as you scale up — and the short answer is: yes, remarkably well. The same induction-adjacent circuitry appears at 1B, 8B, and 70B with near-identical geometry.

The surprising part isn't that circuits exist at every scale. It's that the *basis* transfers: you can take a probe trained on the small model and it lands within a few degrees of the large model's feature directions.

Caveats section is long, and I'd encourage reading it before quote-tweeting the headline figure. Cross-architecture transfer (§5.3) is much weaker than cross-scale transfer.

transformer-circuits.pub ↗
NL Neel Nanda @NeelNanda5 · 07:38

Worth reading alongside ours — we hit the same result from a completely different direction (SAE feature matching rather than probe transfer). When two methods disagree about everything except the conclusion, I update.

GoogleDeepMind @GoogleDeepMind · 06:55

Today we're releasing our study on cross-scale feature universality in language models, with open-sourced SAE weights for all model sizes. Paper and code below.

JB Jack Braun @jbraun_ml · 08:21 · thread, 4 posts

Contrarian take: both papers train on heavily overlapping corpora. "Universal circuits" and "the models memorised the same statistics" are observationally identical here. The cross-architecture weakness in §5.3 is the tell, not a footnote.

What would actually settle this: same experiment on a model trained on disjoint data. Someone with spare compute, please.

LR Lena Rüegg @lenarueegg · 09:55

Wrote up a neutral summary of both papers and the disagreement for people who don't want to read 80 pages: lenarueegg.ch/circuits ↗
